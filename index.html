Record

The Record tool is a centralized record-management system I redesigned for Clemson Computing and Information Technology (CCIT) to make network and infrastructure information easier to find, review, and maintain. The tool allows CCIT staff to browse multiple categories of operational records, search within a category or across the entire system, compare records in a table, view detailed information, and securely add, edit, or delete records when updates are needed. By replacing a large, difficult-to-navigate table-based interface with a searchable and organized workflow, the redesign reduces the time employees spend locating information and makes routine record maintenance more efficient and consistent.
